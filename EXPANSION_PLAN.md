# Comprehensive Quote & Insight Expansion Plan

## 1. Suggestion: New Categories & Books
### **Category: Scale (Revenue & Systems)**
- **"Buy Back Your Time" by Dan Martell:** Perfect for your "Owner vs. Employee" goal. Teaches the "Buyback Loop"—auditing tasks to outsource anything that doesn't provide energy/leverage.
- **"The E-Myth Revisited" by Michael Gerber:** The bible of systemization. Teaches why most small businesses fail because the owner is a "Technician" rather than an "Entrepreneur."
- **"$100M Leads" by Alex Hormozi:** The sequel to Offers. Focuses on the "how" of getting people to see the offer. Essential for the $10k/mo side hustle.

### **Category: Design (Aesthetic & Philosophy)**
- **"Wabi-Sabi for Artists, Designers, Poets & Philosophers" by Leonard Koren:** Matches your "Minimalist/Warm" aesthetic. Teaches finding beauty in the imperfect and transient.
- **"The Design of Everyday Things" by Don Norman:** Fundamental for UX/UI. Will help us refine the MojoHub's frictionless "Flow."
